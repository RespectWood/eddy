# eddy 0.1.0 beta 1

The first test release of **eddy**: click a Spotify link anywhere on your Mac (Messages, Mail, Slack…), and the song opens in **Apple Music** or **TIDAL** instead. Every other link goes to your normal browser, untouched.

Thanks for testing! This is a beta: it works well on the developer's Mac, and your feedback decides what gets fixed before 1.0.

## Install

1. Download **eddy-0.1.0-beta.1-macos.zip** below, unzip it and move **eddy** to Applications. Needs macOS 13 or later.
2. Open eddy. macOS blocks it the first time, because this beta isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
3. In the setup window:
   - choose **Apple Music** or **TIDAL**;
   - TIDAL: click **Connect** and log in, for exact matches;
   - pick your normal browser;
   - click **Use eddy for links** and confirm.
4. Optional: turn on **Start Playing Automatically**. macOS asks for the Accessibility permission.

eddy lives in the menu bar; it has no Dock icon.

## Please try

- Click Spotify links friends send you: in Messages, Mail, Slack, WhatsApp, Notes…
- Ordinary (non-Spotify) links: they should open in your browser as before.
- **Copy Link for Current Song** in eddy's menu: share what you're playing as a TIDAL, Apple Music or Spotify link.
- Autoplay, if you turned it on.

## Report a problem

Open an issue at https://github.com/RespectWood/eddy/issues with:

- your macOS version and eddy's version (menu → **About eddy**);
- the link you clicked (or the song), what you expected and what happened;
- whether you use TIDAL (connected or not) or Apple Music.

Found a security problem? Please report it privately instead, see [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/RespectWood/eddy/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Known limitations

- **TIDAL is in development mode.** All users share one usage limit, so at busy moments eddy may open a TIDAL search instead of the exact song ("TIDAL is rate limiting requests"). If **Connect** doesn't work for your account, TIDAL also opens a search; please mention it in an issue.
- Apple Music is less tested than TIDAL, especially autoplay.
- Spotify links you click *inside* your browser stay in the browser; eddy only sees links from other apps.
- Artist, playlist and podcast links aren't converted yet; they open as usual.

## Privacy

eddy has no server and no analytics; nothing is sent to the developer. Details: [README → Privacy](https://github.com/RespectWood/eddy#privacy).

## Uninstall

Set your normal browser back in **System Settings → Desktop & Dock → Default web browser**, then quit eddy and delete it. Details: [README → Uninstall](https://github.com/RespectWood/eddy#uninstall).
